English

Runic Weaver is a paid, offline, single-player game. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, social feature or third-party network SDK. It does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising identifiers.

Campaign progress and language, music and sound preferences are stored locally on the device. This local data never leaves the device through the app. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Google Play or Apple's App Store processes the purchase and may process payment, account, device or diagnostic information under its own privacy policy. Runic Weaver does not receive payment-card details. Store-platform processing is not controlled by the app developer.

Runic Weaver is intended for a general teen and adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. The app knowingly collects no information from children or adults.

If a future update changes these practices, this policy and the relevant store disclosure will be updated before release. Questions can be sent to the contact address above.
